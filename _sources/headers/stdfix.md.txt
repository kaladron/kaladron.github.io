# stdfix.h

Columns list the 12 fixed-point suffixes: `uhr`/`hr` (`unsigned`/`signed short fract`), `ur`/`r` (`unsigned`/`signed fract`), `ulr`/`lr` (`unsigned`/`signed long fract`), and `uhk`/`hk`, `uk`/`k`, `ulk`/`lk` for the corresponding `accum` types.  See {doc}`../math/stdfix` for compiler requirements, precision macros, formatted I/O specifiers, and ABI notes.
